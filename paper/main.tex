by structured interference that overlaps modulation cues in time and

\section{Introduction}
\IEEEPARstart{A}{utomatic} modulation classification enables a receiver to
infer the modulation of an unknown waveform without transmitter-side control
information.  It supports spectrum monitoring, blind receiver configuration,
interference diagnosis, and adaptive signal processing.  Deep AMC models have
advanced from convolutional classifiers \cite{oshea2016cnn,meng2018amc} to
complex-valued \cite{tu2020complex}, dual-stream \cite{zhang2020dual},
causal-convolutional \cite{tang2024repccnet}, and Transformer-based
\cite{cai2022transformer,shao2025iqformer} representations.  Efficiency,
hardware impairment, and channel robustness have also been studied
\cite{wang2020lightamc,ren2024crosssknet,huang2024csqcc}.

A mobile receiver poses a different failure mode from the usual
additive-white-Gaussian-noise benchmark.  Mobility produces time-varying
multipath and Doppler, while concurrent transmitters and unintended emitters
create narrowband, impulsive, swept, partial-band, and broadband interference.
The target and jammer can therefore share both time and frequency support.
Direct classifiers must learn modulation and nuisance factors from a single
polluted representation.  A hard suppression stage is also unsafe: a cell
with high jammer power may still contain target power.

Recent work narrows individual parts of this gap.  Domain adaptation improves
cross-domain AMC \cite{wang2024sigda,li2026cgpcda}; masked and contrastive
learning improves representation robustness \cite{xiao2024mclhn,
du2025contrastive,wu2026caic}; interference-tolerant feature extraction
directly addresses corrupted observations \cite{sun2025mfenet}; and
overlapped-signal classification studies simultaneous emitters
\cite{jajoo2024overlapped}.  Consequently, neither ``soft masking,''
``contrastive AMC,'' nor ``multi-task AMC'' is a defensible novelty claim by
itself.  What remains insufficiently tested is whether a mask can be assigned
an auditable physical meaning, explicitly retain ambiguous target--jammer
cells, and improve modulation invariance under independently held-out
vehicular and jammer conditions.

This work addresses that question with \method.  It is derived from a broader
interference-adaptation concept, but replaces template matching, binary
component deletion, and rule-based label correction with differentiable,
task-constrained representation allocation.  The paper deliberately does not
claim waveform recovery, strict source separation, protocol identification,
modulation recommendation, or open-set recognition.

The contributions are threefold.
\begin{itemize}
  \item We introduce an environment-conditioned three-way mask on the exact
  complex-STFT lattice.  It allocates target-power-dominant,
  jammer-power-dominant, and unexplained-or-power-ambiguous evidence with a
  learned overlap gate and a bounded additive bypass.
  \item We construct a fixed, component-traceable simulation-component power
  teacher from target, jammer, and unexplained power on that same lattice.
  Jensen--Shannon mask
  supervision and separate modulation, jammer, and link-quality objectives
  make the branch semantics experimentally falsifiable.
  \item During training, we pair two independently impaired observations of
  the same immutable source sequence and apply class-aware exact-source
  contrastive learning; inference uses one view.  The accompanying protocol
  isolates jammer-family, speed, and channel-profile shifts and predeclares
  accuracy, calibration, source-paired significance, diagnostic mechanism
  variables, and complexity.
\end{itemize}

\section{Related Work and Claim Boundary}
\subsection{Deep AMC}
CNN-based AMC established that learned I/Q features can outperform
hand-designed pipelines under matched synthetic conditions
\cite{oshea2016cnn,oshea2017physical,meng2018amc}.  Later work preserved
complex structure \cite{tu2020complex}, fused spatial and temporal streams
\cite{zhang2020dual}, reduced complexity \cite{wang2020lightamc}, and improved
causal or Transformer modeling \cite{tang2024repccnet,cai2022transformer,
shao2025iqformer}.  These models form necessary direct-classification
comparators; architectural depth alone cannot establish interference
disentanglement.

\subsection{Robustness, Masking, and Contrastive Learning}
Channel-robust hand-crafted statistics \cite{huang2024csqcc}, explicit hardware
impairment compensation \cite{ren2024crosssknet}, domain adaptation
\cite{wang2024sigda,li2026cgpcda}, masked contrastive learning
\cite{xiao2024mclhn}, denoising masked autoencoding
\cite{faysal2026denomae}, and cross-SNR contrastive learning
\cite{wu2026caic} address different nuisance sources.  MFENet explicitly
targets interference tolerance \cite{sun2025mfenet}; malicious-interference
recognition has also been studied for MIMO observations
\cite{zhang2026malicious}.  Our narrow distinction is a three-route
component-power teacher on the student's physical lattice, with the other
physical condition of the exact source as the contrastive positive.  Its
target-power-dominant, jammer-power-dominant, and
unexplained-or-power-ambiguous names are task assignments, not identified
sources.  We claim neither the first mask nor the first contrastive AMC model.

\subsection{From Patent Concept to Scientific Hypothesis}
The source concept follows an environment adaptation $\rightarrow$
interference suppression $\rightarrow$ modulation decision chain.  The
scientific implementation makes four material changes.  First, a continuous
environment embedding replaces a discrete jammer template database. Second,
soft three-way allocation replaces hard threshold deletion. Third, a
discriminative classifier replaces nearest-prototype and rule-forced label
revision. Fourth, the jammer state is an auxiliary output and never overrides
the observed modulation class.  This separation prevents a link-adaptation
recommendation prior from being confused with signal identification.

\section{System Model and Learning Problem}
\subsection{Received Signal}
For a length-$N$ complex baseband window, let
\begin{equation}
x[n]=\mathcal{G}_{\eta}\!\left(
\begin{aligned}
&\sum_{\ell=0}^{L_s-1}h_s[n,\ell]s_m[n-\ell]\\
&+\sum_{q=1}^{Q}a_q\sum_{\ell=0}^{L_{j,q}-1}
h_{j,q}[n,\ell]j_q[n-\ell-\tau_q]\\
&+w[n]
\end{aligned}
\right),
\label{eq:system}
\end{equation}
where $s_m$ is a source sequence with modulation label $m$, $h_s$ and
$h_{j,q}$ are independently seeded time-varying channels, $j_q$ is a
structured interferer, and $\mathcal{G}_{\eta}$ collects receiver effects
shared by the complete observation.  Equation~\eqref{eq:system} permits a
general $Q$, but the present evidence protocol admits exactly one structured
jammer family per active view; multi-jammer compositional mixtures are outside
scope.  The generator retains the
post-channel components
\begin{equation}
x=s+j+u,\qquad u=w+a_{\mathrm{rx}},
\label{eq:components}
\end{equation}
after a common gain convention, where $a_{\mathrm{rx}}$ is the tracked
receiver artifact.  Realized powers, rather than requested scale factors,
define
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{SNR}=10\log_{10}\frac{\|s\|_2^2}{\|w\|_2^2},\quad
\mathrm{SIR}=10\log_{10}\frac{\|s\|_2^2}{\|j\|_2^2}.
\label{eq:snrsir}
\end{equation}
No-jammer samples carry a validity mask instead of a finite fictitious SIR.

The main task estimates $p_\theta(m\mid x)$.  Training-only auxiliary targets
are a multi-hot jammer vector and normalized SNR, SIR, and maximum Doppler.
At inference, only $x$ is required.

\subsection{Exact-Source Paired Views}
An immutable source identity contains the modulation, symbol sequence, and
pulse-shaping realization.  For source $i$, two views are
\begin{equation}
x_i^{(v)}=\mathcal{A}\!\left(
s_i;\,h_{s,i}^{(v)},h_{j,i}^{(v)},j_i^{(v)},\eta_i^{(v)}
\right),\quad v\in\{1,2\},
\label{eq:views}
\end{equation}
with independent channel, jammer, receiver, and noise seeds.  All descendants
of one source identity remain in one split.  Thus the pair supplies a valid
training positive without allowing bit-sequence leakage across train,
validation, or test sets.  Only one received view is supplied for each
inference decision; the second view is used during paired training and
source-aligned statistical comparison, not as test-time side information.

\section{\method}
\subsection{Physically Aligned Spectral Front End}
The input is represented as $X=[I;Q]\in\R^{2\times N}$.  A deterministic,
non-centered, full-spectrum complex STFT produces
\begin{equation}
Z=\STFT(I+\mathrm{j}Q)\in\C^{F\times T}.
\end{equation}
The student context encoder receives
\begin{equation}
H=C_\phi\!\left(
\operatorname{stack}\{\Re Z,\Im Z,\log(1+|Z|)\}
\right),
\label{eq:context}
\end{equation}
where $C_\phi$ is a shallow 2-D convolutional residual encoder that preserves
the $F\times T$ mask lattice.  This is a phase-aware real implementation, not
a claim of an end-to-end complex-valued neural network.

\subsection{Environment-Conditioned Tri-Mask}
Mean, standard deviation, and maximum pooling form
\begin{align}
\stats(H)&=[\mu(H),\sigma(H),\max(H)],\\
e_c&=E_\psi\!\left(\stats(H)\right).
\end{align}
Feature-wise conditioning is
\begin{equation}
\widetilde H =
\left(1+\tfrac{1}{2}\tanh\gamma(e_c)\right)\odot H+\beta(e_c).
\label{eq:film}
\end{equation}
The model predicts
\begin{align}
\tau_c &= \tau_{\min}+(\tau_{\max}-\tau_{\min})\sigma(g_\tau(e_c)),\\
[M_s,M_j,M_o]&=\softmax_{\mathrm{route}}\!
\left(G_\omega(\widetilde H)/\tau_c\right),
\label{eq:studentmask}
\end{align}
so the three nonnegative masks sum to one at every time-frequency cell.
Here $M_s$, $M_j$, and $M_o$ denote target-power-dominant,
jammer-power-dominant, and unexplained-or-power-ambiguous allocations,
respectively.  These are component-power task routes, not identified sources.

Two bounded gates allocate the ambiguous route:
\begin{align}
\lambda &=\sigma(g_\lambda(e_c)),\\
\rho&=\rho_{\min}+(\rho_{\max}-\rho_{\min})\sigma(g_\rho(e_c)),\\
Z_m&=(M_s+\lambda M_o+\rho)\odot Z,\label{eq:zmod}\\
Z_j&=(M_j+(1-\lambda)M_o)\odot Z.\label{eq:zjam}
\end{align}
Importantly, $\lambda$, $\rho$, and $\tau_c$ are condition-dependent but are
not constrained to be monotonic in SIR.  Any such relationship must be
measured rather than asserted.

\subsection{Fixed Simulation-Component Three-Route Teacher}
Let $P_s=|\STFT(s)|^2$, $P_j=|\STFT(j)|^2$, and
$P_u=|\STFT(u)|^2$ on the same lattice as $Z$.  Define
\begin{equation}
q_k=\frac{P_k}{P_\Sigma},\quad
P_\Sigma=P_s+P_j+P_u>0,\quad
k\in\{s,j,u\},
\end{equation}
For $v=q_s+q_j>0$, let $r_s=q_s/v$, $r_j=q_j/v$, and $d=r_s-r_j$;
when $v=0$, set $r_s=r_j=d=0$.  The teacher is
\begin{align}
M_s^\star &= v[d]_+,\label{eq:tms}\\
M_j^\star &= v[-d]_+,\label{eq:tmj}\\
M_o^\star &= q_u+v(1-|d|).\label{eq:tmo}
\end{align}
Thus equal target--jammer power is assigned to uncertainty, while receiver
artifacts and noise are always unexplained.  The teacher is deterministic,
has no trainable or exponentially averaged parameters, and is removed at
inference.  A zero-power cell is assigned $(0,0,1)$.  The implementation uses
the per-example guard
\(\epsilon_p=\max(10^{-8}\operatorname{mean}_{f,t}P_\Sigma,
\operatorname{tiny})\): cells with \(P_\Sigma\leq\epsilon_p\) are assigned
\((0,0,1)\), all other denominators are lower-bounded for numerical safety,
and every cell is finally renormalized to the simplex.  Thus the proposition
below describes the ideal nonempty definition; the finite-precision
implementation deliberately maps negligible-energy cells to uncertainty.

\begin{proposition}[Simplex and common-scale invariance]
For nonnegative component powers, \eqref{eq:tms}--\eqref{eq:tmo} are
nonnegative and sum to one wherever $P_\Sigma>0$; the stipulated zero-power
assignment is also on the simplex.  In exact arithmetic, multiplying all
three complex components by a common nonzero scalar leaves the masks
unchanged.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Since $r_s+r_j=1$, $|d|\leq1$.  Hence all three terms are nonnegative.
Furthermore,
\begin{align*}
M_s^\star+M_j^\star+M_o^\star
&=v|d|+q_u+v(1-|d|)\\
&=v+q_u=q_s+q_j+q_u=1.
\end{align*}
A common complex scale multiplies every $P_k$ by the same positive constant,
which cancels in $q_k$ and all downstream quantities.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Phase preservation]
For a real nonnegative mask $W[f,t]$, the masked coefficient
$\widetilde Z[f,t]=W[f,t]Z[f,t]$ has
$\angle\widetilde Z[f,t]=\angle Z[f,t]$ whenever $W[f,t]>0$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A positive real scalar changes only the magnitude of a complex number.  At
$W=0$ the coefficient is suppressed and phase is undefined.
\end{proof}
The lemma motivates the phrase ``phase-preserving allocation.'' It does not
imply perfect reconstruction or source identifiability.

\subsection{Dual Branches and Exact-Source Contrast}
Independent spectral encoders yield $z_m=P_m(Z_m)$ and $z_j=P_j(Z_j)$.
A linear head predicts modulation from $z_m$; a multi-label head predicts
jammer presence from $z_j$; and a quality head predicts normalized SNR, valid
SIR, and Doppler from $[e_c;z_j]$.

For a batch of paired views, let $\bar z_i^{(1)}$ and $\bar z_i^{(2)}$ be
unit-normalized modulation embeddings.  The valid comparison set for anchor
$i$ is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{A}_i=\{i\}\cup\{k:y_k\neq y_i\}.
\end{equation}
The diagonal is the other view of the exact same source; other sources of the
same modulation are ignored.  One direction of the loss is
\begin{equation}
\ell_i^{1\rightarrow2}=
-\log
\frac{\exp(\bar z_i^{(1)\top}\bar z_i^{(2)}/\tau_x)}
{\sum_{k\in\mathcal{A}_i}
\exp(\bar z_i^{(1)\top}\bar z_k^{(2)}/\tau_x)}.
\label{eq:xcc}
\end{equation}
The cross-condition loss averages both directions.  Unlike generic supervised
contrastive learning \cite{khosla2020supcon}, it neither treats all same-class
signals as positives nor turns a different source of the same class into a
false negative.  The current method does not claim jammer- or SIR-aware hard
negative mining.

\subsection{Objective and Curriculum}
The full objective is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}=L_{\rm mod}
+\lambda_jL_{\rm jam}
+\lambda_qL_q
+\lambda_mL_{\rm mask}
+\lambda_xL_{\rm xcc}
+\lambda_\perp L_\perp,
\label{eq:loss}
\end{equation}
where $L_{\rm mask}=\JS(M^\star\|M)$, $L_q$ is validity-masked smooth
$L_1$, and $L_\perp$ is the squared centered cosine similarity between active
jammer-task and modulation-task embeddings.  This orthogonality term is
evaluated only for jammer-active views and does not establish source
identifiability.  The locked initial weights are
$(\lambda_j,\lambda_q,\lambda_m,\lambda_x,\lambda_\perp)=
(0.25,0.05,0.50,0.10,0.01)$ with label smoothing $0.05$ and
$\tau_x=0.12$.  Mask and contrastive terms ramp in after the classification
warm-up. Learning-rate decay and checkpoint selection begin only after the
complete objective has been active for a minimum number of epochs, preventing
early validation checkpoints from bypassing the proposed losses.

\section{Simulation Evidence Protocol}
\subsection{Data Layers}
The evidence is intentionally layered.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item A deterministic Python proxy generator is used for unit tests,
  diagnostics, and inexpensive screening.  Its channel heuristics cannot
  support a standards-compliance claim.
  \item The frozen headline-cache builder is configured to use MATLAB
  \texttt{nrTDLChannel} primitives corresponding to TDL-A through TDL-E in
  3GPP TR~38.901
  \cite{3gpp38901}. Target and jammer waveforms traverse independent channel
  objects before shared receiver effects and exact realized SNR/SIR scaling.
  TDL-A/C/D are allocated to training and validation; TDL-B/E are reserved for
  a profile-held-out test.
\end{enumerate}
TDL profiles alone do not instantiate every geometry, blockage, or nonstationary
trajectory in a V2X evaluation methodology \cite{3gpp37885}.  We therefore use
the narrower term ``TR~38.901 TDL-profile simulations.''  This paper makes no
claim about SDR captures, field deployment, complete V2X scenario compliance,
or engineering transfer.

\subsection{Signal, Jammer, and Impairment Space}
The locked target set contains BPSK, $\pi/2$-BPSK, QPSK, 8PSK, 16QAM, 64QAM,
256QAM, GMSK, CPFSK, and 4FSK.  The seen jammer dictionary contains tone,
multitone, chirp, sweep, partial-band, and comb waveforms; pulse and OFDM-like
interference are held out by family.  Modulated cochannel collisions and
multi-jammer compositional mixtures are excluded from the single-label
headline protocol.  Here, \emph{cochannel} means an in-taxonomy modulated
cochannel collision, whereas \emph{mixed} means a composition of multiple
jammer families.  Without a target anchor, swapping two in-taxonomy cochannel
emitters can leave
the observation distribution unchanged while changing the requested label.
On a balanced source-exchange class with identical \(x\) and two required
labels, the mixture-only Bayes error is therefore at least \(1/2\).
Such collisions require a dominant-emitter label, an explicit physical anchor,
or a separately scored ambiguity task.  The exact discrete SNR grid for every
split is
\(\{-10,-6,-2,2,6,10,14,18\}\) dB.  Jammer-active ordinary splits use
SIR \(\in\{-10,-5,0,5,10\}\) dB, while the hard-interference split uses
SIR \(\in\{-15,-10,-5,0\}\) dB.  Clean samples have invalid SIR by
construction.  Training samples are drawn only from these declared discrete
grids; there is no continuous training interpolation.

Receiver factors in the present implementation include carrier-frequency and
phase offset, I/Q gain/phase imbalance, additive DC offset, and window-level
AGC normalization.  The multiplicative linear effects are shared across
target, jammer, and noise so that the component identity in
\eqref{eq:components} remains auditable; DC is tracked in the unexplained
component.  Timing/sampling offset, receiver filtering, clipping, and
quantization are outside the current evidence scope.

\subsection{Comparators and Ablations}
All trainable models receive identical source pairs, split manifests, epoch
budget, optimizer family, label smoothing, and checkpoint policy.  Literature
models are labelled ``reimplementation,'' ``inspired,'' or ``supervised
adaptation'' according to their provenance unless official code and the
complete published protocol are executed unchanged.  Quoted paper numbers are
never mixed with unified retraining.

The A2--A5 chain adds one predeclared intervention block at each step under
the shared protocol: the fixed teacher at A3--A2, the bundled jammer, quality,
and orthogonality objectives at A4--A3, and exact-source contrast at A5--A4.

composite control because route count, capacity, and overlap allocation change

\subsection{Metrics and Statistical Inference}
Primary outcomes are macro-F1 and accuracy; secondary outcomes are worst
supported-class recall, negative log-likelihood, and 15-bin expected
calibration error.  Source-aligned prediction bundles retain per-source
SNR/SIR metadata for a separately preregistered stratified analysis.  The
current release reports only frozen regime aggregates and makes no released
full-SNR--SIR-plane claim.

The primary reference comparator is predeclared before any model training or
test evaluation and is not selected on validation.  For each frozen source
set, models produce source-aligned probability bundles.  Candidate--reference
accuracy and macro-F1 differences use a 10,000-draw, class-stratified
source-cluster paired bootstrap.  For the five-seed headline comparison, the
primary hierarchical paired bootstrap resamples both algorithm seeds and the
same paired source clusters; source-only and seed-only intervals are also
reported.  An exact two-sided McNemar test is supplemental, restricted to one
fitted-model seed, reported for every predeclared candidate, and never computed
by pooling predictions across seeds.  Holm adjustment is applied within each
predeclared held-out-regime \(\times\) seed family across the candidate model
family; it does not correct across regimes or seeds and is not the basis for
the headline inference.  Validation is excluded from testing.  Screening uses
three model seeds, while the locked headline comparison uses five.

\subsection{Mechanism Tests}
Accuracy alone cannot verify the proposed interpretation.  Let
$W_m=M_s+\lambda M_o+\rho$.  For independently tracked target and jammer
spectra, define
\begin{align}
\alpha&=\frac{\|W_m\odot Z_s\|_2^2}{\|Z_s\|_2^2},&
\beta&=\frac{\|W_m\odot Z_j\|_2^2}{\|Z_j\|_2^2},\\
\Delta_{\rm spec}&=10\log_{10}\frac{\alpha}{\beta}.
\label{eq:mechanism}
\end{align}
We report energy-weighted route correlation and MAE against the teacher,
route occupancy, $\alpha$, $\beta$, and $\Delta_{\rm spec}$.  Because the
additive bypass can make $W_m>1$, $\alpha$ is a target-energy transfer ratio,
not a bounded retention rate; its mean, maximum, and fraction of samples with
$\alpha>1$ are reported.  The teacher's third route combines unexplained
power and target--jammer power ambiguity, which are not identifiable as
separate constituents from the student mask.  Their teacher-side occupancies
may be tabulated only to describe the simulated target; correlation and MAE
are computed for the combined route.  A 1,000-draw permutation test evaluates
the diagnostic association between predicted and teacher third-route
occupancy.  These mechanism probes are exploratory diagnostic associations
outside the headline multiplicity family.  In particular,
\(\Delta_{\rm spec}\) is an oracle-conditioned spectral component ratio, not
waveform SIR gain, source separation, or a real-world SIR estimator.

The auxiliary heads are evaluated directly: jammer micro/macro F1 and AUROC,
and validity-masked SNR, SIR, and Doppler MAE after conversion back to physical
units.  Undefined AUROC or unavailable scaling metadata is reported as
unavailable rather than imputed.

\subsection{Complexity}
Parameter count and serialized state size are exact.  Convolution/linear MACs
exclude the STFT, whose real-operation count is reported separately from the
standard FFT order estimate.  Batch-one P50/P95 latency is reported only for
the frozen device used by the eligible run after warm-up and required device
synchronization.  The operator must isolate that device before measurement;
the release records the device, runtime, window length, and synchronization
policy.  No CPU--GPU comparison or cross-device latency claim is made.

\section{Reproducibility, Limitations, and Scope}
Each executed run is required to store the complete configuration, environment
record, dataset
manifest, digest, model seed, checkpoint, source-aligned probabilities,
per-regime metrics, and paired statistics.  Student initialization occurs
after seeding.  Validation selection is delayed until the full objective is
active.  Tables are populated from eligible machine-readable artifacts; the
paper has no manually typed performance cells.

Three limitations are structural.  First, the teacher requires clean component
bookkeeping during simulation training. It cannot be applied to unreferenced
field captures, where mask supervision must be disabled. Second, TDL channels
are standards-aligned primitives rather than a complete nonstationary V2X
geometry; the manuscript therefore makes a simulation-only claim. Third,
separately computed component STFT powers omit
coherent cross terms and therefore define a simulation-component allocation
target, not a conserved decomposition of mixture energy.  The masks allocate
task representations and do not reconstruct isolated time-domain sources; the
additive bypass makes this distinction explicit.

\section{Conclusion}
This paper formulates interference-robust AMC under vehicular-Doppler
TDL-profile simulations as physically supervised representation allocation.
\method\ combines a target-power-dominant/jammer-power-dominant/
unexplained-or-power-ambiguous teacher, dual task branches, and exact-source
cross-condition learning on a shared complex-STFT lattice.  Its simulation
claims are deliberately falsifiable through source-disjoint factor holds,
single-view inference with source-paired statistics, and oracle-conditioned
diagnostic component probes.
