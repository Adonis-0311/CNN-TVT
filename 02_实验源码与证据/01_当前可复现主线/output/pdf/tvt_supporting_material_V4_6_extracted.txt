Abstract
This is optional supporting material. No result relied upon by the manuscript appears only in
this document. The two preregistered severe-held robustness validations are stated in the manuscript
and expanded here only at artifact level; neither changes the original sealed evidence class. The
purpose is to expose the artifact-level detail behind those results, so that a reviewer who wants to
audit the construction of the evaluation cells, the full capacity-control sweep, the per-SNR comparator-
fidelity curves, the covariate ladder and selector audits, the leave-−15-dB-out extrapolation and paired
overlap-increment tests, the comparator convergence histories, the sidecar architecture, or the seed-
level robustness can do so directly. It also reports two preregistered post-hoc robustness validations on
an independently generated severe held set: a transfer check that falsifies the severe-corner advantage
and the envelope’s−15-dB transport, and a repair check showing that the received-I/Q sidecar restores
the compact model on the same held set.
I. SCOPE ANDHOW TOREADTHISDOCUMENT
The study asks why compact spectral and high-capacity I/Q AMC representations exhibit condition-
dependent rank instability, how far a campaign-internal empirical envelope transports, and whether a
representation intervention can repair the failure exposed by independent severe-domain validation.
All evidence is from source-disjoint 3GPP TR 38.901 TDL-profile simulation.
Because this document is standalone, the identifiers used throughout are restated here.
•A0is the direct spectral reference backbone (8,458 parameters) andA5is the sealed complete
configuration (39,500 parameters), which adds a three-route spectral allocation, a simulation-
component teacher, multi-task and contrastive objectives, a residual bypass, and a small route-
orthogonality regularizer.
•S,M, andLare prospective spectral capacity tiers. Sisthe sealed A5 configuration; M and L
widen only the spectral-channel, embedding, and environment dimensions, to 99,596 and 233,244
parameters.
•sidecaris a prospective received-I/Q repair branch that raises the total model size from 39,500
to 46,794 parameters.
•IQFormer-inspiredandMCLDNNare architecture-faithful unified-retraining references, not
exact reproductions of every original training recipe.
•Thetarget-support jammer overlapois the fraction of the jammer’s total STFT power that falls
inside the target’s 99%-energy time–frequency support. It is not an occupied-bandwidth fraction.
Four evidence levels are kept apart. The sealed family contains the original confirmatory contrasts.
Campaign-internal SNR–SIR, envelope, fusion, and capacity analyses are exploratory. Probe, coverage,
route-gating, and sidecar experiments are prospective diagnosis or intervention evidence. The two later

II. CONSTRUCTION OF THEEVALUATIONCELLS
The envelope is fitted on aggregate cells rather than on individual windows. A cell is one (split,
SIR stratum, overlap quartile) triple that retains at least 150 windows and all ten modulation classes.
Overlap quartile edges are computed within each split. Table I gives the resulting construction; the fit
total of 32 and the primary held total of 80 are both reconstructible from its rows.
Two features of Table I are worth stating plainly. First, theid_testsplit contributes 16 cells rather
than 20 because its lowest overlap quartile is populated almost entirely by jammer-free windows, which
carryo= 0and the SIR= 0dB cache sentinel; that bin clears the 150-window floor only in the
0dB stratum. Second, the singleclean_retentioncell is excluded from the primary envelope
because a jammer-free window has no physical finite SIR, which is also why its window count is the
full split rather than a quartile of it.

IV. CLEAN-SENTINELFITTING-CELLSENSITIVITY
The primary held envelope excludes jammer-free clean retention because a jammer-free window has
no physical finite SIR. The fitting domain, however, retains one aggregate dominated by jammer-free
sentinel windows, theid_test/SIR= 0/lowest-overlap cell (mean overlap≈10 −11, 1053 windows,
SIR taken from the cache sentinel). To close this definition asymmetry, we exclude exactly that
aggregate, refit the two-variable envelope on the remaining 31 cells, and re-score the unchanged 80
finite-SIR interfered held cells; no model prediction changes and nothing is retrained.
The excluded aggregate is theid_test/SIR= 0/lowest-overlap cell dominated by jammer-free
sentinel windows. Excluding it moves the held RMSE by at most0.20percentage points andR 2
skill
by at most0.05, leaves both coefficient signs and the break-even ordering (required overlap rising as
interference becomes less severe) unchanged, and therefore leaves the envelope conclusions materially
unchanged; the 32-cell primary fit is retained.

VI. PROSPECTIVETIER-2 INTERVENTIONS
The Tier-2 chain was designed after inspecting the frozen evidence and sits outside the sealed
family. Table VI restates its three arms with the intervals that the compact form in the manuscript
omits.
The ordering carries the argument. Adding jammer-free windows for every class moves clean macro-
F1 by0.15percentage points, so exposure alone is not the explanation. Gating the modulation route on
predicted interference presence moves nothing either, and the learned gate saturates near the modulated
route in both clean and hard conditions (99.994%and99.991%), so it functions as a negative control
rather than as a demonstration that a mask fallback was learned. Only restoring a small amount of
received-I/Q information moves both conditions with intervals clear of zero.

VIII. COMPARATORFIDELITY ON APUBLICBENCHMARK
This check exists to establish that the two I/Q comparators are implemented competently, not to
validate the simulator-domain envelope; a public benchmark cannot do the latter, because it does
not expose the separately tracked target and jammer components the overlap covariate requires. The
protocol follows the benchmark result table released with the IQFormer reference implementation: the
11-class RML2016.10a set, the full−20to18dB grid, per-stratum600/200/200splits seeded at233,
three algorithm seeds, no augmentation and no input-length change. The public-benchmark fidelity runs
use a separate recipe following the benchmark protocol—a60-epoch budget for IQFormer-inspired
and a200-epoch budget for MCLDNN—and should not be used to infer convergence sufficiency of
the30-epoch main campaign.

IX. COMPARATORCONVERGENCEAUDIT
All models were trained under one shared 30-epoch budget with the same cosine-scheduled learning
rate and an 8-epoch early-stopping rule; the reported checkpoint is selected solely by validation
modulation cross-entropy. Table VIII audits the sealed training histories (selected epoch and completed
epochs over the ten algorithm seeds) to check whether any comparator’s comparison is distorted by
an immediately binding training cap.
The budget binds asymmetrically. IQFormer-inspired and CSSL run the full 30-epoch budget in all
ten seeds (median selected epoch 27 and 29), so their reported performance should be interpreted as
potentially budget-limited under this shared 30-epoch protocol and the shared epoch budget may favor
faster-converging models; the manuscript states this caveat in its Limitations. MCLDNN early-stops
in every seed with best epochs well inside the budget (median 13, range 11–16), so the severe-region
comparisons against MCLDNN are not explained by a binding cap. A5 itself also trains to the budget
in all ten seeds, and the direct spectral reference A0 likewise runs to the budget in all ten seeds (median
selected epoch 30, range 28–30), so the cap concern is not one-sided against the comparators. No
model was retrained for this audit; the table reads the sealed histories only.
X. SIDECARARCHITECTUREDETAIL
Table IX lists the received-I/Q sidecar layer by layer so the reported parameter increment is fully
reconstructible. All convolution layers are bias-free; SiLU activations (parameter-free) follow each
normalization. The branch consumes the received two-channel mixture at the full sample rate, uses
one depthwise-separable stack with aggressive time pooling (mean/std/max over the window), and
fuses the resulting embedding residually with the spectral embedding before a side classification
head. The fused embedding passes through the 10-class linear head of layer 13, and these logits are
added residually to the base spectral classifier logits; the side head receives no separate auxiliary
supervision, and inference consumes only the received mixture. The parameter counts were verified
directly against the sealed checkpoint state dict.

cells (sign accuracy1/4; realized cell-mean macro-F10.285versus the0.361hindsight oracle on
those four cells). The cross-model selector therefore transfers poorly to the excluded severe stratum,
which is the selector-side counterpart of the extrapolation caveat above.
XIII. INDEPENDENTSEVERE-HELDVALIDATION: TRANSFERFAILURE OF THECORNER
ADVANTAGE AND THEENVELOPE
After the campaign was frozen, a preregistered post-hoc robustness validation asked whether the
severe-corner advantage and the overlap–SIR envelope survive on data generated outside the frozen
campaign. A new severe held set was simulated with fresh source IDs that are disjoint from the
campaign source pool (12,820 new sources against the campaign’s 152,000; the disjointness is
asserted programmatically at cache build), at the two severe regimes and view0, and stored under
standards/cache_s5_severe_held_v1/with a manifest recording the cache digest. All
frozen checkpoints were evaluated unchanged; no model was retrained, tuned, or selected against this
set. The validation is exploratory and post-hoc relative to the sealed family throughout.
Table XIV reports the preregistered transfer verdicts. The pooled A5 gain is negative against both
comparators, the envelope’s sign accuracy is near chance (and below it against MCLDNN), a SIR-only
or constant predictor beats the full overlap–SIR envelope on RMSE against both references, and the
overlap–SIR cell selector recalls only 0.581 of the truly A5-favoring cells. The preregistered verdict
is therefore a transfer failure: neither the severe-corner advantage nor the−15-dB envelope transport
reproduces on independently generated severe data.

XIV. INDEPENDENTSEVERE-HELDVALIDATION: REPRESENTATIONREPAIR BY THEI/Q
SIDECAR
A second preregistration, frozen before any inference on the new held set, then asked whether the
received-I/Q sidecar repairs the failure exposed above. The data stay locked to the same cache; the A5,
IQFormer-inspired, and MCLDNN predictions are reused from the transfer check; and the ten sidecar
checkpoints pass a provenance audit (46794 parameters each, strict loading of the frozen state dicts,
SHA-256 recorded indocs/S5_R1_CHECKPOINT_AUDIT.md). The primary contrast is sidecar
minus A5, pooled as the equally weighted mean of the per-seed paired differences, with a seed-level
paired bootstrap interval.
The repair is robust. Pooled macro-F1 rises from 20.72% (A5) to 25.09% (sidecar), a difference
of 4.37 percentage points ([2.96,5.90]), with regime contributions 4.22 and 4.52 percentage points;
the difference is positive in 10 of 10 seeds and in 8 of eight SNR strata. A window-level hierarchical
paired audit reproduces the interval. On the same held set the sidecar also overtakes IQFormer-inspired
(23.29%; descriptive gap-recovery ratio 1.70) while recovering only 0.34 of the remaining gap to
MCLDNN (33.75%). The preregistered verdict is therefore a strong repair effect: the representation
repair generalizes beyond the campaign even where the campaign-internal corner advantage and the
envelope transport do not. Per-seed, per-SNR, and per-class tables are stored as CSV (artifact index
below); the sidecar remains exploratory and outside the sealed family.
