An Occupancy-Indexed Operating Envelope for Compact
Physics-Guided Automatic Modulation Classification under
Structured Interference
Anonymous Author(s)
Abstract—Uniform model rankings hide a central design ques-
tion in automatic modulation classification (AMC): when does a
compact physics-guided representation beat a much larger I/Q-
domain classifier under structured interference? We study this
question in source-disjoint 3GPP TR 38.901 TDL-profile simula-
tions using VIMD-Net, a 39500-parameter spectral network with
a simulation-component teacher, three soft allocation routes, and
exact-source cross-condition training. A two-variable empirical
envelope fitted only on in-distribution and hard-interference cells
relates its gain to jammer spectral occupancy and signal-to-
interference ratio (SIR). Against an IQFormer-inspired reference,
the envelope attains 0.975 sign agreement over 81 cells from
unseen regimes. At SIR=−15dB, the compact model gains 9.74
percentage points over MCLDNN and 10.46 percentage points
over IQFormer-inspired, with positive paired intervals and the
same direction in every seed. The sealed confirmatory method
effect over the shared backbone is 4.57 percentage points with a
simultaneous interval of[3.65,5.49]percentage points; however,
the confirmatory family gate and the clean-retention gate did not
pass. Frozen-checkpoint probes and prospective Tier-2 experi-
ments localize the clean boundary to a repairable representation
gap: adding a 46794-parameter received-I/Q sidecar improves
clean macro-F1 by 11.77 percentage points and hard-interference
macro-F1 by 4.37 percentage points. These Tier-2 results are
exploratory, outside the frozen confirmatory family. The paper
therefore reports a simulation-only operating envelope and a
repair path, not uniform superiority.
Index Terms—Automatic modulation classification, structured
interference, vehicular Doppler, operating envelope, representa-
tion allocation, compact neural networks.
Evidence status.The sealed primary method contrast is positive, but
the confirmatory family gate and clean-retention gate did not pass.
Post-hoc stratification is explicitly labeled. Tier-2 probe, repair, and
capacity results are exploratory and outside the frozen confirmatory
family. Every numerical value is generated from a source artifact; the
scope is simulation only.
I. INTRODUCTION
A
UTOMATIC modulation classification supports spectrum
monitoring and blind receiver configuration by inferring
a waveform’s modulation from received samples. Deep AMC
has progressed from convolutional I/Q classifiers [1], [2] to
complex-valued, multi-stream, recurrent, and Transformer-like
representations [3]–[7]. Compact architectures remain attrac-
tive when inference cost matters [8], but average accuracy
alone does not say where a compact representation is prefer-
able.
Structured interference makes that omission consequential.

frequency. The same model can trail a high-capacity I/Q

a severe, spectrally occupied corner. Averaging these regimes
produces a ranking but erases the design rule.
This paper asks a narrower question:can the sign and size
of the compact-model advantage be indexed by physical con-
dition variables, and can the boundary revealed by that index
be repaired?We answer using a frozen source-disjoint simu-
lation campaign, artifact-only post-hoc analysis, and prospec-
tive Tier-2 experiments. The base model, VIMD-Net, maps
a complex STFT to target-dominant, jammer-dominant, and
unexplained-or-ambiguous routes. Its teacher is available only
during simulation training and is derived from independently
tracked target, jammer, and residual components.
The contributions are:
•an occupancy–SIR operating envelope that unifies gains
and losses and predicts their sign outside the regimes used
to fit it;
•a statistically reinforced severe-interference result in
which the 39500-parameter model exceeds 354984–
406070-parameter baselines, together with full-region
maps that prevent selective reporting;
•a sealed confirmatory backbone-relative effect and an
honest bounded conclusion for component-level interven-
tions whose joint family gate did not pass;
•a condition-transfer diagnosis and prospective repair
chain showing that clean exposure alone is insufficient,
while a small received-I/Q sidecar repairs both clean and
hard-interference performance; and
•a traceable evidence layer in which manuscript numbers
and figures resolve to immutable artifact files and selec-
tors.
The claim is deliberately not that VIMD-Net is uniformly
best. It is that compact physics-guided spectral allocation has a
reproducible operating region, that the region can be predicted
from physical covariates, and that its principal clean boundary
is architectural rather than an unavoidable cost of compactness.

CNN and recurrent AMC systems learn directly from I/Q
sequences [1], [2], [5]; later work preserves complex struc-
ture, fuses temporal and spatial streams, or applies attention
[3], [4], [6], [7]. Channel adaptation, masked or contrastive
learning, and interference-tolerant features address different

nuisance sources [9]–[12]. These papers motivate strong I/Q-
domain comparators and prevent us from presenting masking
or contrastive learning alone as novelty.
The 2024–2026 frontier also includes overlapped-signal
classification, denoising masked autoencoding, cross-domain
prototypical adaptation, and a tensor method for malicious
interference in MIMO systems [13]–[16]. Their signal models
and information contracts are not interchangeable with our
frozen jammer-factor protocol, so we cite them for positioning
rather than importing published numbers into the unified-
retraining table. Accordingly, our claim is an audited operating
boundary and repair chain under controlled simulation, not
universal state of the art.
Time–frequency masks are established separation tar-
gets. Binary, ratio, phase-sensitive, complex-ratio, and deep-
clustering formulations have been studied extensively [17]–
[21]. Our use differs in purpose: the mask allocates evidence
for classification and does not reconstruct sources. The third
route retains unexplained power and target–jammer ambiguity
that a suppression system might otherwise discard.
The main distinction of this work is evaluative. Rather than
infer uniform superiority from a marginal score, we compare
compact spectral and larger I/Q representations across an
explicit physical plane, estimate an empirical boundary, test it
on held-out regimes, and then intervene on the boundary. All
evidence is restricted to TR 38.901 TDL-profile simulations.
No claim is made about complete V2X geometry, waveform
recovery, source separation, open-set recognition, or hardware
deployment.
III. SYSTEMMODEL ANDMETHOD
A. Received Signal and Paired Views
For source sequences[n]and jammeru[n], one received
window is
x[n] =R(H s(s)[n] +γH u(u)[n] +w[n]),(1)
whereH s andH u are independently drawn fading and mo-
bility operators,γcontrols SIR,wis receiver noise, andR
applies the frozen receiver chain. Training pairs two indepen-
dently impaired observations of the same immutable source.
Inference uses one view. Pairing is therefore a training and
statistical-alignment device, not additional test-time informa-
tion.
Unanchored in-taxonomy cochannel collisions are excluded.
Without a physical target anchor, exchanging two admissible
emitters can preserve the mixture while changing the requested
single label, so that task requires a different label definition.
B. Three-Route Representation Allocation
LetZ= STFT(x)∈C F×T . The front end receives
real, imaginary, and log-magnitude planes and produces logits
As,A j,A o on the same lattice. The allocation masks are
[Ms,M j,M o] = softmax([As,A j,A o]),
Ms +M j +M o = 1. (2)
An environment encoder produces a scalar ambiguous-route
gateλand a bounded bypassρ. The modulation and jammer
routes are
Zm = (Ms +λM o +ρ)⊙Z,(3)
Zj = (Mj + (1−λ)M o)⊙Z.(4)
Because these weights are real and nonnegative, every retained
coefficient keeps the mixture STFT phase. This elementary
property does not imply source identifiability.
C. Simulation-Component Teacher
During training only, independently tracked post-channel
target, jammer, and residual components define powers
Ps,P j,P u on the student’s lattice. Withq k =P k/(Ps +P j +
Pu), the fixed teacher is

The routes are nonnegative and sum to one. Common scaling
cancels in the ratios. Negligible-energy cells are assigned to
the uncertainty route by the finite-precision implementation.
Jensen–Shannon supervision aligns student and teacher masks;
modulation, jammer-family, and link-quality objectives con-
strain route semantics; and an exact-source contrastive loss
encourages condition invariance. The teacher is not needed at
inference.
IV. EVIDENCEPROTOCOL
A. Frozen Simulation Campaign
The artifact-locked campaign contains 12 model variants,
10 algorithm seeds, 120 fits, 11 evaluation splits, and 100000
training source sequences. Target classes cover PSK, QAM,
continuous-phase, and FSK families. Structured jammer fami-
lies span tone, multitone, chirp, partial band, pulse, broadband,
and out-of-taxonomy modulated sources. TDL-A/C/D profiles
are seen during training, while TDL-B/E profiles support held-
channel evaluation. Jammer family, speed, channel, combined
OOD, receiver stress, and clean-retention splits share source
identities across model comparisons.
Comparators include a shared direct spectral backbone (A0),
MCLDNN, an IQFormer-inspired classifier, and an official-
architecture CSSL supervised adaptation. The full method
is A5. Ablations alter route count, teacher form, multi-task
terms, contrastive learning, and the residual bypass. Strong
comparators are anchors, not claims of exact reproduction of
every original training recipe.
B. Statistical Separation of Evidence Classes
The sealed confirmatory family contains three hard-
interference contrasts: the complete method versus A0, the
margin teacher versus a proportional-power teacher, and the
tri-route model versus a dual-route control. A joint non-
studentized max-absolute-deviation hierarchical paired boot-
strap supplies simultaneous intervals over seeds and class-
stratified source clusters. The family gate requires every si-
multaneous lower bound to exceed zero.

V. RESULTS
A. Confirmed Whole-Method Effect, Bounded Component Ef-
fects
Table I reports the complete confirmatory family. The
whole method improves over its shared A0 backbone by 4.57
percentage points, with a simultaneous interval of[3.65,5.49]
percentage points. This effect is about three times the design
resolution of 1.31 percentage points. The teacher-form and
route-count interventions do not clear zero individually; under
the same joint interval their contributions are bounded within
1.36 percentage points. Thus the architecture-level effect is
resolved but cannot be assigned to either isolated component
at this resolution. Because the family rule required all three
contrasts, the family gate did not pass.
At the same parameter tier, the residual-free A7 variant
is nominally above A5 in the marginal hard-interference
aggregate (Fig. 5). The difference is well below the design
resolution and is consistent with the bounded component-level
result. We therefore attribute the compact frontier position to
the VIMD family at this budget, not uniquely to the bypass.

F . Capacity Ladder Separates Size from Representation
Finally, we widen the spectral model without changing its
representation domain. The S, M, and L tiers contain 39500,
99596, and 233244 parameters. Relative to S, M improves hard
macro-F1 by 1.94 percentage points and L by 3.18 percentage
points. L also improves over M by 1.24 percentage points.
Capacity therefore improves average fitting.
Nevertheless, L remains below IQFormer-inspired
on overall hard interference by -2.84 percentage
points([−4.30,−1.31]). At SIR=−15dB, the same L
model is ahead by 14.07 percentage points([9.55,18.51]).
Increasing spectral capacity therefore shifts performance but
does not remove the operating boundary. The result supports
an architecture/representation interpretation rather than a pure
parameter-count explanation.
VI. DISCUSSION
The evidence suggests a practical model-selection rule. A
compact spectral front end is most attractive when interference
is both severe and occupies enough of the time–frequency
plane for learned allocation to matter. A larger I/Q model is
preferable in weak, clean, or low-occupancy conditions unless
the compact system includes an I/Q side channel. The two
models are also complementary enough that late probability
fusion can outperform either alone.
The sequence of interventions matters scientifically. The
frozen probe narrows the cause; the coverage arm rejects
a cheap explanation; the I/Q sidecar repairs the predicted
missing information; and the capacity ladder shows that width
alone does not eliminate the boundary. This chain is more
informative than a single improved score because each neg-
ative or positive outcome discriminates between competing
explanations.
The envelope is intentionally empirical. Occupancy and SIR
are correlated in the current design, jammer occupancy is
estimated from simulator-tracked components, and the law has
only been tested on regimes contained in the frozen cache.
Its value is as a compact prediction and audit device. A
future prospective campaign should randomize occupancy and
SIR more independently and estimate deployment-available
occupancy proxies.
VII. LIMITATIONS ANDREPRODUCIBILITY

are from source-disjoint TR 38.901 TDL-profile simulations,
not a complete vehicular geometry. Second, the teacher uses
simulation-component bookkeeping and cannot supervise un-

clean-retention gate did not pass; only the complete A5–A0
contrast is a positive sealed confirmatory result. Fourth, the

Reproducibility is enforced at the artifact boundary. Each
manuscript macro stores the source path, row selector, ev-

rendered from CSV files and carry a separate provenance
manifest. The frozen run stores cache identity, configura-

overwriting an unfavorable sealed gate.
VIII. CONCLUSION
Compact physics-guided AMC and high-capacity I/Q AMC
exchange rank across the structured-interference plane. An
occupancy–SIR law predicts that exchange outside its fit
regimes, and a post-hoc but statistically reinforced severe
corner shows a reproducible advantage for the compact model.
The sealed whole-method effect over a shared backbone is
positive, while two isolated component effects are bounded
and the confirmatory family gate does not pass. The clean-
retention gate also does not pass, but the probe–coverage–
sidecar chain localizes and repairs the representation gap;
the capacity ladder shows that parameter count alone cannot
explain the remaining boundary. The result is an honest
simulation-only operating envelope with a concrete repair path,
not a claim of uniform superiority.
