\begin{abstract}
This is optional supporting material. No result required to support the
manuscript's main claims appears only in this document. It provides complete
cell construction, aggregation definitions, per-seed/SNR/class results,
architecture details, frozen-control analyses, convergence histories,
prospective-freeze provenance, and artifact paths for results summarized in the
manuscript. The three prospectively frozen severe-held stages---the transfer
check, the sidecar repair, and the frozen-inference attribution controls---are
stated in the manuscript and expanded here only at artifact level; none
changes the original sealed evidence class. A reviewer who wants to audit the
construction of the evaluation cells, the full capacity-control sweep, the
per-SNR comparator-fidelity curves, the covariate ladder and selector audits,
the leave-$-15$-dB-out extrapolation and paired overlap-increment tests, the

construction and control breakdowns, or the seed-level robustness can do so
directly.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope and How to Read This Document}
The study asks why compact spectral and high-capacity I/Q AMC representations
exhibit condition-dependent rank instability, how far a campaign-internal
empirical envelope transports, and whether a joint representation-and-fusion
intervention can repair the degradation exposed by independent severe-domain
validation. All
evidence is from source-disjoint 3GPP TR~38.901 TDL-profile simulation.

Because this document is standalone, the identifiers used throughout are
restated here.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{A0} is the direct spectral reference backbone (8{,}458
  parameters) and \textbf{A5} is the sealed complete configuration
  (39{,}500 parameters), which adds a three-route spectral allocation, a
  simulation-component teacher, multi-task and contrastive objectives, a
  residual bypass, and a small route-orthogonality regularizer.
  \item \textbf{S}, \textbf{M}, and \textbf{L} are prospective spectral
  capacity tiers. S \emph{is} the sealed A5 configuration; M and L widen only
  the spectral-channel, embedding, and environment dimensions, to
  99{,}596 and 233{,}244 parameters.
  \item \textbf{sidecar} is a prospective received-I/Q repair branch that
  raises the total model size from 39{,}500 to 46{,}794 parameters.
  \item \textbf{IQFormer-inspired} and \textbf{MCLDNN} are
  architecture-faithful unified-retraining references, not exact reproductions
  of every original training recipe.
  \item The \textbf{target-support jammer overlap} $o$ is the fraction of the
  jammer's total STFT power that falls inside the target's 99\%-energy
  time--frequency support. It is not an occupied-bandwidth fraction.
\end{itemize}

Four evidence levels are kept apart. The sealed family contains the original
confirmatory contrasts. Campaign-internal SNR--SIR, envelope, fusion, and
capacity analyses are exploratory. Probe, coverage, route-gating, and sidecar
experiments are prospective diagnosis or intervention evidence. The severe-held
transfer check, the sidecar repair, and the frozen-inference attribution
controls are three sequential prospectively frozen post-campaign diagnostic
stages on one independently generated held set; their freeze provenance is in
Section~\ref{sec:preregp}. None changes the original sealed family.

\section{Campaign-Diagnostic Envelope Skill by Held Regime}
Table~\ref{tab:regime} reports the held-regime skill for both strong
references, including two diagnostics that are easy to omit and easy to
misread. ``Const.'' is the fit-domain constant predictor: the mean gain over
the 32 fitting cells, applied unchanged to the held cells, with
$R^2_{\mathrm{skill}}=1-\mathrm{MSE}_{\mathrm{envelope}}/\mathrm{MSE}_{\mathrm{const}}$.
``Oracle'' is instead the mean of the held cells themselves (pooled row) or of
the regime's own held cells (regime rows), which no predictor could know in
advance; it is reported as a hindsight reference, not as evidence that the
envelope fails to resolve within-regime structure.

The heterogeneity is real and is not smoothed away. Against
IQFormer-inspired, the envelope predicts the gain level well in the
unseen-speed and held-out-channel regimes ($R^2_{\mathrm{skill}}=0.75$ and

and 5.41 vs 8.41 pp against MCLDNN), while on the unseen-jammer and
combined-OOD shifts the regime-specific constant is better. The pooled oracle
(6.04 and 6.81 pp) is lower than the pooled envelope RMSE because it
uses the held-domain mean in hindsight; a pooled constant still bears the
between-regime variation and the pooled comparison should not be read as the
envelope lacking within-regime structure. The demonstrated property is
therefore regime-dependent transport and within-regime structure, not uniform
held-domain superiority.

retains one aggregate dominated by jammer-free sentinel windows, the
\texttt{id\_test}/SIR$=0$/lowest-overlap cell (mean overlap

close this definition asymmetry, we exclude exactly that aggregate, refit

\section{Seed-Level Robustness}
The sealed intervals treat algorithm seeds as fixed blocks. Treating them
instead as a random effect and resampling seeds directly gives
$[4.02,5.19]$~\pp\ for the A5--A0 whole-configuration contrast,
$[-0.15,0.42]$~\pp\ for the teacher-form contrast, $[-0.15,1.04]$~\pp\ for the
route-count contrast, and $[-0.19,0.62]$~\pp\ for the residual-free A7 variant
against A5. The qualitative reading is unchanged under either treatment: the
whole-configuration effect is resolved, the isolated component effects are not.

\section{Sidecar Architecture Detail}
Table~\ref{tab:sidecar} lists the received-I/Q sidecar layer by layer so the
reported parameter increment is fully reconstructible. All convolution layers
are bias-free; SiLU activations (parameter-free) follow each normalization.

with the spectral embedding before a side classification head. The fused
embedding passes through the 10-class linear head of layer~13, and these
logits are added residually to the base spectral classifier logits; the side
head receives no separate auxiliary supervision, and inference consumes only
the received mixture. The parameter
counts were verified directly against the sealed checkpoint state dict.

\section{Independent Severe-Held Validation: Transfer Boundary of the Corner
Advantage and the Diagnostic Envelope}
\label{sec:transfer}
After the campaign was frozen, a prospectively frozen post-campaign robustness
validation asked whether the severe-corner advantage and the overlap--SIR
envelope survive on data generated outside the frozen campaign. The
independent severe-held set fixes SIR at $-15$~dB and uses two
source-disjoint regimes with different operating-regime factors: regime R1
keeps the training channel profiles (TDL-A/C/D) and combines them with held
jammer families (pulse and OFDM-like emitters) at a held terminal speed of
$180$~km/h, while regime R2 combines held channel profiles (TDL-B/E) with the
same held jammer families at $250$~km/h. Each regime contains
\SevereHeldSourcesPerRegime\ fresh sources disjoint from the campaign source
pool (\SevereHeldSourcesTotal\ new sources against the campaign's 152,000; the disjointness is
asserted programmatically at cache build), evaluated on the eight-point SNR
grid from $-10$ to $18$~dB at view $0$. The set is stored under
\path{standards/cache_s5_severe_held_v1/} with a manifest recording the
cache digest. All frozen checkpoints were evaluated unchanged; no model was
retrained, tuned, or selected against this set. The validation is
exploratory and post-campaign relative to the sealed family throughout.

Two aggregation conventions appear in this document, and every reported
number is labelled by its convention. \emph{Window-pooled} macro-F1 pools all
predictions first and then computes macro-F1; it is used for the transfer
gains in Table~\ref{tab:s5a}. The \emph{seed-wise} convention computes
macro-F1 per seed and per regime first and then averages, with pooled the
equally weighted regime mean; it is used for the repair contrast and levels
and for all frozen-inference controls, and it is the manuscript's headline
convention because it supports paired seed inference. Because macro-F1 is
nonlinear, the two conventions need not agree numerically
($\SevereTransferGainIqformer$ versus $\SevereSeedMeanGapIqformer$ \pp\
against IQFormer-inspired, $\SevereTransferGainMcldnn$ versus
$\SevereSeedMeanGapMcldnn$ \pp\ against MCLDNN for the A5 gap on this held
set), and they are never mixed within a single contrast.

Table~\ref{tab:s5a} reports the frozen transfer verdicts. The pooled
A5 gain is negative against both comparators, the envelope's sign accuracy
is near chance (and below it against MCLDNN), a SIR-only or constant
predictor beats the full overlap--SIR envelope on RMSE against both
references, and the overlap--SIR cell selector recalls only
\SevereTransferSelectorRecall\ of the truly A5-favoring cells. The
frozen verdict is therefore a transfer failure: neither the
severe-corner advantage nor the $-15$-dB envelope transport reproduces on
independently generated severe data.

\section{Independent Severe-Held Validation: Representation Repair by the
I/Q Sidecar}
After the prospectively frozen transfer check revealed a cross-regime transport
boundary on the independently generated severe-held set, a second analysis
plan froze the repair hypothesis, primary contrast, and decision
rule before any sidecar inference on that same held set. The data stay locked
to the same cache; the A5, IQFormer-inspired, and MCLDNN predictions are
reused from the transfer check; and the ten sidecar checkpoints pass a
provenance audit (\SidecarParameters\ parameters each, strict loading of the
frozen state dicts, SHA-256 recorded in
\path{docs/S5_R1_CHECKPOINT_AUDIT.md}). The primary contrast is sidecar
minus A5 under the seed-wise convention, pooled as the equally weighted mean
of the per-seed paired differences, with a seed-level paired bootstrap
interval.

The repair is consistent across seeds and strata. Seed-mean pooled macro-F1
rises from \SevereRepairLevelAFive\%
(A5) to \SevereRepairLevelSidecar\% (sidecar), a difference of
\SevereRepairDiff\ \pp\ ($[\SevereRepairCiLow,\SevereRepairCiHigh]$), with
regime contributions \SevereRepairRegimeOneDiff\ and
\SevereRepairRegimeTwoDiff\ \pp; the difference is positive in
\SevereRepairPositiveSeeds\ of \ProtocolSeedCount\ seeds and in
\SevereRepairPositiveSnrStrata\ of eight SNR strata. A window-level
hierarchical paired audit reproduces the interval. On the same held set the
sidecar also overtakes IQFormer-inspired (\SevereRepairLevelIqformer\%;
descriptive gap-recovery ratio \SevereRepairGapRecoveryIqformer) while
recovering only \SevereRepairGapRecoveryMcldnn\ of the remaining gap to
MCLDNN (\SevereRepairLevelMcldnn\%). The absolute levels remain low for every
evaluated model at this SIR $=-15$~dB operating point, so the verdict is a
consistent relative repair effect rather than deployable classification
accuracy. The repair transfers to the independently generated severe-held
regimes even where the campaign-internal corner advantage and the envelope
transport do not. Per-seed, per-SNR, and per-class tables are stored as CSV
(artifact index below); the sidecar remains exploratory and outside the
sealed family.

attribution controls on the
same locked cache before any control inference
(\path{docs/S6_SEVERE_HELD_INFERENCE_CONTROLS_PREREG.md}). No model was
retrained, tuned, or re-selected; the controls are post-campaign diagnostic
evidence and cannot change the sealed family or the two earlier severe-held
verdicts.

Control~A forwards the frozen capacity tiers M (\CapacityMParameters\
parameters) and L (\CapacityLParameters\ parameters) on both regimes, with
A5 and the sidecar restricted to the same matched five seeds
(17, 29, 43, 71, 101). Tested spectral width scaling does not reproduce the
repair: M and L reach \SevereControlLevelCapM\% and \SevereControlLevelCapL\%

including zero), whereas on the matched subset the sidecar reaches
\SevereControlMatchedLevelSidecar\% against
\SevereControlMatchedLevelAFive\% for A5.

replaces the I/Q branch input with zeros of the same shape; B2 applies a
fixed random temporal permutation of the 1024 samples within each window,
identical across the two channels, which preserves the marginal sample
distribution but destroys modulation temporal structure. Both interventions
degrade the sidecar substantially, to \SevereControlLevelIqZero\% and
\SevereControlLevelIqShuffle\%, losses of \SevereControlIqZeroLoss\ and
\SevereControlIqShuffleLoss\ \pp\ relative to the full sidecar.

$\mathrm{classifier}(\mathrm{fused\ embedding})$ and keeps the co-trained
base spectral logits. The sidecar falls to \SevereControlLevelNoSideHead\%
($\SevereControlDiffNoSideHead$ \pp\ against A5), below A5 itself, so the
added fusion and classifier path is necessary for the repair.
