\begin{abstract}
This is optional supporting material. It is written to be read on its own; the
manuscript it accompanies states every result it relies on and points here
only for audit-level tables. The purpose is narrower: to expose the
artifact-level detail behind those results, so that a reviewer who wants to
audit the construction of the evaluation cells, the full capacity-control
sweep, the per-SNR comparator-fidelity curves, the covariate ladder and
selector audits, the leave-$-15$-dB-out extrapolation and paired
overlap-increment tests, the comparator convergence histories, the sidecar
architecture, or the seed-level robustness can do so directly. It also
reports two preregistered post-hoc robustness validations on an
independently generated severe held set: a transfer check that falsifies the
severe-corner advantage and the envelope's $-15$-dB transport, and a repair
check showing that the received-I/Q sidecar restores the compact model on
the same held set. Nothing in this
document introduces a new claim, a new experiment, or a new evidence class.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope and How to Read This Document}
The study behind this material asks when a compact spectral classifier and a
much larger I/Q-domain classifier exchange rank under structured interference,
describes that exchange with a two-variable empirical envelope, and then
diagnoses and repairs the representation boundary the exchange reveals. All
evidence is from source-disjoint 3GPP TR~38.901 TDL-profile simulation.

Because this document is standalone, the identifiers used throughout are
restated here.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{A0} is the direct spectral reference backbone (8{,}458
  parameters) and \textbf{A5} is the sealed complete configuration
  (39{,}500 parameters), which adds a three-route spectral allocation, a
  simulation-component teacher, multi-task and contrastive objectives, a
  residual bypass, and a small route-orthogonality regularizer.
  \item \textbf{S}, \textbf{M}, and \textbf{L} are prospective spectral
  capacity tiers. S \emph{is} the sealed A5 configuration; M and L widen only
  the spectral-channel, embedding, and environment dimensions, to
  99{,}596 and 233{,}244 parameters.
  \item \textbf{sidecar} is a prospective received-I/Q repair branch that
  raises the total model size from 39{,}500 to 46{,}794 parameters.
  \item \textbf{IQFormer-inspired} and \textbf{MCLDNN} are
  architecture-faithful unified-retraining references, not exact reproductions
  of every original training recipe.
  \item The \textbf{target-support jammer overlap} $o$ is the fraction of the
  jammer's total STFT power that falls inside the target's 99\%-energy
  time--frequency support. It is not an occupied-bandwidth fraction.
\end{itemize}

Two evidence classes are kept apart everywhere. The \emph{sealed} family is the
original frozen confirmatory set; everything else, including the envelope, the
severe-interference stratification, the Tier-2 interventions, and the capacity
ladder, is \emph{exploratory or post-hoc} and cannot change a sealed
conclusion.

\section{Envelope Skill by Held Regime}
Table~\ref{tab:regime} reports the held-regime skill for both strong
references, including two diagnostics that are easy to omit and easy to
misread. ``Const.'' is the fit-domain constant predictor: the mean gain over
the 32 fitting cells, applied unchanged to the held cells, with
$R^2_{\mathrm{skill}}=1-\mathrm{MSE}_{\mathrm{envelope}}/\mathrm{MSE}_{\mathrm{const}}$.
``Oracle'' is instead the mean of the held cells themselves (pooled row) or of
the regime's own held cells (regime rows), which no predictor could know in
advance; it is reported as a hindsight reference, not as evidence that the
envelope fails to resolve within-regime structure.

The heterogeneity is real and is not smoothed away. Against
IQFormer-inspired, the envelope transports the gain level well into the
unseen-speed and held-out-channel regimes ($R^2_{\mathrm{skill}}=0.75$ and

(3.94 vs 6.71 and 4.86 vs 6.95 pp against IQFormer-inspired; 4.66 vs 7.78
and 5.41 vs 8.41 pp against MCLDNN), while on the unseen-jammer and
combined-OOD shifts the regime-specific constant is better. The pooled oracle
(6.04 and 6.81 pp) is lower than the pooled envelope RMSE because it
uses the held-domain mean in hindsight; a pooled constant still bears the
between-regime variation and the pooled comparison should not be read as the
envelope lacking within-regime structure. The demonstrated property is
therefore regime-dependent transport and within-regime structure, not uniform
held-domain superiority.

\section{Seed-Level Robustness}
The sealed intervals treat algorithm seeds as fixed blocks. Treating them
instead as a random effect and resampling seeds directly gives
$[4.02,5.19]$~\pp\ for the A5--A0 whole-configuration contrast,
$[-0.15,0.42]$~\pp\ for the teacher-form contrast, $[-0.15,1.04]$~\pp\ for the
route-count contrast, and $[-0.19,0.62]$~\pp\ for the residual-free A7 variant
against A5. The qualitative reading is unchanged under either treatment: the
whole-configuration effect is resolved, the isolated component effects are not.

\section{Sidecar Architecture Detail}
Table~\ref{tab:sidecar} lists the received-I/Q sidecar layer by layer so the
reported parameter increment is fully reconstructible. All convolution layers
are bias-free; SiLU activations (parameter-free) follow each normalization.
The branch consumes the received two-channel mixture at the full sample rate,
uses one depthwise-separable stack with aggressive time pooling
(mean/std/max over the window), and fuses the resulting embedding residually
with the spectral embedding before a side classification head. The fused
embedding passes through the 10-class linear head of layer~13, and these
logits are added residually to the base spectral classifier logits; the side
head receives no separate auxiliary supervision, and inference consumes only
the received mixture. The parameter

ladder that isolates the skill of each physical covariate and (ii) a
model-selection selector evaluation. No model is retrained and no prediction
changes; both are exploratory.

whereas SIR and overlap have comparable single-covariate skill against

envelope---well supported against MCLDNN, not validated against
IQFormer-inspired---rather than independently held-regime-validated
thresholds.

\section{Independent Severe-Held Validation: Transfer Failure of the Corner
Advantage and the Envelope}
After the campaign was frozen, a preregistered post-hoc robustness
validation asked whether the severe-corner advantage and the overlap--SIR
envelope survive on data generated outside the frozen campaign. A new severe
held set was simulated with fresh source IDs that are disjoint from the
campaign source pool (12,820 new sources against the campaign's 152,000;
the disjointness is asserted programmatically at cache build), at the two
severe regimes and view $0$, and stored under
\path{standards/cache_s5_severe_held_v1/} with a manifest recording the
cache digest. All frozen checkpoints were evaluated unchanged; no model was
retrained, tuned, or selected against this set. The validation is
exploratory and post-hoc relative to the sealed family throughout.

Table~\ref{tab:s5a} reports the preregistered transfer verdicts. The pooled

references, and the overlap--SIR cell selector recalls only
\SevereTransferSelectorRecall\ of the truly A5-favoring cells. The
preregistered verdict is therefore a transfer failure: neither the
severe-corner advantage nor the $-15$-dB envelope transport reproduces on

\section{Independent Severe-Held Validation: Representation Repair by the
I/Q Sidecar}
A second preregistration, frozen before any inference on the new held set,
then asked whether the received-I/Q sidecar repairs the failure exposed
above. The data stay locked to the same cache; the A5, IQFormer-inspired,
and MCLDNN predictions are reused from the transfer check; and the ten
sidecar checkpoints pass a provenance audit (\SidecarParameters\ parameters
each, strict loading of the frozen state dicts, SHA-256 recorded in
\path{docs/S5_R1_CHECKPOINT_AUDIT.md}). The primary contrast is sidecar
minus A5, pooled as the equally weighted mean of the per-seed paired
differences, with a seed-level paired bootstrap interval.

The repair is robust. Pooled macro-F1 rises from \SevereRepairLevelAFive\%
(A5) to \SevereRepairLevelSidecar\% (sidecar), a difference of
\SevereRepairDiff\ \pp\ ($[\SevereRepairCiLow,\SevereRepairCiHigh]$), with
regime contributions \SevereRepairRegimeOneDiff\ and
\SevereRepairRegimeTwoDiff\ \pp; the difference is positive in
\SevereRepairPositiveSeeds\ of \ProtocolSeedCount\ seeds and in
\SevereRepairPositiveSnrStrata\ of eight SNR strata. A window-level
hierarchical paired audit reproduces the interval. On the same held set the
sidecar also overtakes IQFormer-inspired (\SevereRepairLevelIqformer\%;
descriptive gap-recovery ratio \SevereRepairGapRecoveryIqformer) while
recovering only \SevereRepairGapRecoveryMcldnn\ of the remaining gap to
MCLDNN (\SevereRepairLevelMcldnn\%). The preregistered verdict is therefore
a strong repair effect: the representation repair generalizes beyond the
campaign even where the campaign-internal corner advantage and the envelope
transport do not. Per-seed, per-SNR, and per-class tables are stored as CSV
(artifact index below); the sidecar remains exploratory and outside the
sealed family.
