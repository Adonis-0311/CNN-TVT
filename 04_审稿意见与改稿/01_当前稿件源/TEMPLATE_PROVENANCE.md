unused-option warning. The local class is vendored so a future TeX
installation update cannot silently change the submission layout. The paper
retains only packages needed by its own equations, tables, figures, and
internal evidence lock; sample-only content is not copied.

Before upload, the human authors must still confirm any journal-specific
submission options against the live IEEE TVT author instructions.
