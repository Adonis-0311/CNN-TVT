Current official guidance checked on 2026-08-17:

- [TVT Instructions for Authors](https://vtsociety.org/publication/ieee-transactions-vehicular-technology/guidelines-authors/instructions): initial Regular Papers up to 14 pages; revised/final versions up to 16 pages; double-column, at least 10-point; references and biographies count.
- [TVT FAQ](https://vtsociety.org/publication/ieee-transactions-vehicular-technology/guidelines-authors/frequently-asked-questions): PDF is preferred and page-limit details are repeated.
- [TVT page charges](https://vtsociety.org/publication/ieee-transactions-vehicular-technology/guidelines-authors/instructions/page-charges): mandatory overlength charge begins after 10 printed pages for a Regular Paper; a VTS-member corresponding author may receive the benefit described there from 1 July 2026.
- [TVT scope](https://vtsociety.org/files/ieeevts/2025-03/Scope%20of%20the%20IEEE%20Transactions%20on%20Vehicular%20Technology.pdf): includes digital modulation/transmission and radio-frequency interference within the Communications area.

Page-limit compliance does not imply scientific acceptance or waive fees.
